## Consultation policy

For coding tasks, delegate to consultor before architectural or public contract
changes, after two substantive unsuccessful attempts at one blocker, and after a
substantial implementation milestone with validation evidence. Explicit user
requests also apply. Small tasks need no consultation unless a trigger applies.
Send a focused question, evidence and constraints; wait and assess the response.
Avoid repeated consultation without new evidence and recursive delegation.
Model and effort are in ~/.codex/agents/consultor.toml (respect CODEX_HOME).
Prefer the custom consultor role. If only explicit model/effort delegation is
available, read that file and pass its settings and instructions. If neither is
available, report the limitation. Never simulate a second-model consultation.
